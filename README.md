## One of my favorite series is Suits
Suits is a legal drama show about a genius college dropout named Mike Ross
who accidentally gets hired as an associate at a top New York law firm.
Even though he has a photographic memory and knows everything about the law,
he doesn't actually have a law degree, which is completely illegal.
Together with his confident boss, Harvey Specter,
Mike has to win big court cases while constantly trying to keep his secret from being exposed to the rest of the firm.
